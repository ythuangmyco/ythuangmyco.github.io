I completed my PhD at NKUST, Taiwan, in 2026, specializing in enzyme biotechnology, focusing on cellulase production, purification, optimization, and immobilization. My research includes enzyme enhancement, nanomaterial-based immobilization, and fungal biomass for heavy-metal removal. Currently, I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at NCKU, focusing on the development of nanocarrier-based strategies against biofilms formed by the emerging pathogenic fungi *Scedosporium apiospermum* and *Lomentospora prolificans*. This research integrates materials science, drug delivery, and medical mycology to improve antifungal penetration and therapeutic efficacy.
