Hi, I am Yu Qing Lin. I am currently studying soil microbial communities in Taiwan by using soil DNA to analyze differences in microbial populations. Previously, I focused on aspergillosis, investigating the relationship between spore germination rates and high-risk patients. Outside of research, I enjoy traveling and exploring different places.
